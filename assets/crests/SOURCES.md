# Crest sources

**[Replaced 2026-11-28, A332.]** Every crest here is now the site's own artwork, not a
franchise's official logo. Until that date this folder held the official logos -- fifteen
from Wikipedia's file pages and three supplied by the owner (Kings XI Punjab 2008-2019,
Royal Challengers Bangalore 2008-2019, Delhi Daredevils) -- listed in this file's history.

Team NAMES are still trademarks of their franchises and appear only to identify the teams;
the site is not affiliated with or endorsed by the BCCI, the IPL or any franchise.

Each file here is the SOURCE for one crest; `tools.build_crests` makes the served copies.
Stored as lossless WebP, cropped to content and capped at 640px. Which season each crest
serves is decided in `web/crests.py`, not here.

## Supplied by the site owner (2026-11-28), stated to be copyright-free

image (white, or a checkerboard), removed by flood fill from the edges over light,
near-grey pixels, with the cut edge softened by a pixel and isolated specks under 400
pixels dropped. Rising Pune Supergiant and Kochi Tuskers Kerala had real transparency and
were cropped only.
