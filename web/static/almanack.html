The Almanack

The record, ball by ball

The Almanack is the IPL's record from every ball bowled since 2008: 1,243 matches and 816 players. Every figure on these pages is worked out from the ball-by-ball archive the game itself is built on. Each player's page measures phase and pace-or-spin figures against the league in the same seasons, so a strike rate from 2009 and one from 2025 can be read side by side.

Did you know

  • AB de Villiers has been named Player of the Match 25 times, more than anyone else.
  • Aaron Finch has played for nine different franchises, the most of anyone.
  • Three players have played in all 19 seasons: Manish Pandey, Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli.
  • Glenn Maxwell has been out for a duck 19 times, the most of anyone.
  • Chris Gayle has hit 357 sixes, the most in the IPL.
  • The highest score is Chris Gayle's 175* off 66 balls against Pune Warriors on 23 April 2013.
  • The best bowling figures are Alzarri Joseph's 6/12 against Sunrisers Hyderabad on 6 April 2019.
