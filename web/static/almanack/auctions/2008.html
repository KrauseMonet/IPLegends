The 2008 auction

The sale

The 2008 auction, held before the 2008 season, sold 89 players for $38.04m in all. The dearest buy was MS Dhoni, to Chennai Super Kings for $1.5m. Auctions to 2013 were conducted in US dollars, and the prices here are as they were bid.
