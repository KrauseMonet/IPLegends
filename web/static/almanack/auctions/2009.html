The 2009 auction

The sale

The 2009 auction, held before the 2009 season, sold 17 players for $7.65m in all. The dearest buy was Andrew Flintoff, to Chennai Super Kings for $1.55m. Auctions to 2013 were conducted in US dollars, and the prices here are as they were bid.
