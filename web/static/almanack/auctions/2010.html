The 2010 auction

The sale

The 2010 auction, held before the 2010 season, sold 11 players for $3.65m in all. The dearest buy was Kieron Pollard, to Mumbai Indians for $750,000. Auctions to 2013 were conducted in US dollars, and the prices here are as they were bid.
