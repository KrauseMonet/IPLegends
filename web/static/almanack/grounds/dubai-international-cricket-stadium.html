The ground

Dubai International Cricket Stadium in Dubai has staged 46 IPL matches in 3 seasons (2014 to 2021). Most often it has been the home of Delhi Capitals and Chennai Super Kings. A full twenty-over first innings here has averaged 164 runs. Of 43 matches decided outright, the side batting second won 22 (51%). The side that won the toss went on to win 18 of 46 (39%).
