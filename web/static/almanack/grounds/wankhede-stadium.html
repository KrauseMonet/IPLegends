The ground

Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai has staged 132 IPL matches in 16 seasons (2008 to 2026). Most often it has been the home of Mumbai Indians. A full twenty-over first innings here has averaged 173 runs. Of 131 matches decided outright, the side batting second won 72 (55%). The side that won the toss went on to win 69 of 132 (52%).
