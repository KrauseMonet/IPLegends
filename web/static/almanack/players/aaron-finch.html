The career

Aaron Finch is a batter from Australia who played 92 IPL matches in 11 seasons (2010 to 2022), for Rajasthan Royals, Delhi Capitals, Pune Warriors, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Mumbai Indians, Gujarat Lions, Punjab Kings, Royal Challengers Bengaluru and Kolkata Knight Riders. Aaron Finch scored 2,091 runs at a strike rate of 128.2 and an average of 25.19. Named Player of the Match five times.
