The career

AB de Villiers is a batter from South Africa who played 184 IPL matches in 14 seasons (2008 to 2021), for Delhi Capitals and Royal Challengers Bengaluru. AB de Villiers scored 5,162 runs at a strike rate of 151.7 and an average of 39.71, the 10th-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match 25 times.
