The career

Ajinkya Rahane is a batter from India who played 214 IPL matches in 18 seasons (2008 to 2026), for Mumbai Indians, Rajasthan Royals, Rising Pune Supergiant, Delhi Capitals, Kolkata Knight Riders and Chennai Super Kings. Ajinkya Rahane scored 5,367 runs at a strike rate of 125.6 and an average of 30.15, the 8th-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match 13 times.
