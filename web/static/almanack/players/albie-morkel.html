The career

Albie Morkel is a bowler from South Africa who played 90 IPL matches in 9 seasons (2008 to 2016), for Chennai Super Kings, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals and Rising Pune Supergiant. Albie Morkel took 85 wickets at an economy of 8.21, the 45th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Albie Morkel scored 974 runs at a strike rate of 142.0 and an average of 24.35. Named Player of the Match twice.
