The career

Ambati Rayudu is a batter from India who played 204 IPL matches in 14 seasons (2010 to 2023), for Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings. Ambati Rayudu scored 4,348 runs at a strike rate of 127.5 and an average of 28.23, the 18th-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match ten times.
