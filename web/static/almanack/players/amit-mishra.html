The career

Amit Mishra is a bowler from India who played 162 IPL matches in 16 seasons (2008 to 2024), for Delhi Capitals, Deccan Chargers, Sunrisers Hyderabad and Lucknow Super Giants. Amit Mishra took 174 wickets at an economy of 7.38, the 10th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Amit Mishra scored 381 runs at a strike rate of 90.9 and an average of 11.91.
