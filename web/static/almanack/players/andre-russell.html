The career

Andre Russell is an all-rounder from West Indies who played 139 IPL matches in 13 seasons (2012 to 2025), for Delhi Capitals and Kolkata Knight Riders. Andre Russell scored 2,651 runs at a strike rate of 174.2 and an average of 28.20, the 40th-most runs in the IPL's history. Andre Russell took 123 wickets at an economy of 9.51, the 22nd-most wickets in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match 16 times.
