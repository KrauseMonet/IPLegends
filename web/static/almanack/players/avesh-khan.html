The career

Avesh Khan is a bowler from India who played 82 IPL matches in 10 seasons (2017 to 2026), for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals, Lucknow Super Giants and Rajasthan Royals. Avesh Khan took 93 wickets at an economy of 9.28, the 36th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match three times.
