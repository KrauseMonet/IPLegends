The career

Bhuvneshwar Kumar is a bowler from India who played 206 IPL matches in 16 seasons (2011 to 2026), for Pune Warriors, Sunrisers Hyderabad and Royal Challengers Bengaluru. Bhuvneshwar Kumar took 226 wickets at an economy of 7.71, the 2nd-most wickets in the IPL's history. Bhuvneshwar Kumar scored 354 runs at a strike rate of 93.9 and an average of 9.32. Named Player of the Match seven times.
