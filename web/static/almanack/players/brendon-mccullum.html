The career

Brendon McCullum is a batter from New Zealand who played 110 IPL matches in 11 seasons (2008 to 2018), for Kolkata Knight Riders, Kochi Tuskers Kerala, Chennai Super Kings, Gujarat Lions and Royal Challengers Bengaluru. Brendon McCullum scored 2,880 runs at a strike rate of 131.7 and an average of 27.69, the 33rd-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match five times.
