The career

Chris Gayle is a batter from West Indies who played 141 IPL matches in 13 seasons (2009 to 2021), for Kolkata Knight Riders, Royal Challengers Bengaluru and Punjab Kings. Chris Gayle scored 4,965 runs at a strike rate of 149.0 and an average of 39.72, the 11th-most runs in the IPL's history. Chris Gayle took 18 wickets at an economy of 7.90. Named Player of the Match 22 times.
