The career

Chris Morris is a bowler from South Africa who played 81 IPL matches in 8 seasons (2013 to 2021), for Chennai Super Kings, Rajasthan Royals, Delhi Capitals and Royal Challengers Bengaluru. Chris Morris took 95 wickets at an economy of 8.01, the 35th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Chris Morris scored 618 runs at a strike rate of 155.3 and an average of 22.07. Named Player of the Match three times.
