The career

Dale Steyn is a bowler from South Africa who played 95 IPL matches in 11 seasons (2008 to 2020), for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Deccan Chargers, Sunrisers Hyderabad and Gujarat Lions. Dale Steyn took 97 wickets at an economy of 6.92, the 32nd-most wickets in the IPL's history. Dale Steyn scored 167 runs at a strike rate of 104.4 and an average of 7.59. Named Player of the Match five times.
