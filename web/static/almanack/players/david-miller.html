The career

David Miller is a batter from South Africa who played 156 IPL matches in 15 seasons (2012 to 2026), for Punjab Kings, Rajasthan Royals, Gujarat Titans, Lucknow Super Giants and Delhi Capitals. David Miller scored 3,288 runs at a strike rate of 139.5 and an average of 35.74, the 28th-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match five times.
