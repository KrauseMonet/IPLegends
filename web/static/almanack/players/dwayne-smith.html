The career

Dwayne Smith is an all-rounder from West Indies who played 91 IPL matches in 9 seasons (2008 to 2017), for Mumbai Indians, Deccan Chargers, Chennai Super Kings and Gujarat Lions. Dwayne Smith scored 2,385 runs at a strike rate of 135.2 and an average of 28.39, the 48th-most runs in the IPL's history. Dwayne Smith took 26 wickets at an economy of 9.02.
