The career

Faf du Plessis is a batter from South Africa who played 155 IPL matches in 14 seasons (2011 to 2025), for Chennai Super Kings, Rising Pune Supergiant, Royal Challengers Bengaluru and Delhi Capitals. Faf du Plessis scored 4,773 runs at a strike rate of 135.8 and an average of 35.10, the 14th-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match ten times.
