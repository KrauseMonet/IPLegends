The career

Gautam Gambhir is a batter from India who played 154 IPL matches in 11 seasons (2008 to 2018), for Delhi Capitals and Kolkata Knight Riders. Gautam Gambhir scored 4,217 runs at a strike rate of 123.9 and an average of 31.01, the 20th-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match 13 times.
