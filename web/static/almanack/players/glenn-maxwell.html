The career

Glenn Maxwell is a batter from Australia who played 141 IPL matches in 13 seasons (2012 to 2025), for Delhi Capitals, Mumbai Indians, Punjab Kings and Royal Challengers Bengaluru. Glenn Maxwell scored 2,819 runs at a strike rate of 155.1 and an average of 23.89, the 36th-most runs in the IPL's history. Glenn Maxwell took 41 wickets at an economy of 8.30. Named Player of the Match nine times.
