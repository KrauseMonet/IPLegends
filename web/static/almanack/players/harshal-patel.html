The career

Harshal Patel is a bowler from India who played 123 IPL matches in 14 seasons (2012 to 2026), for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals, Punjab Kings and Sunrisers Hyderabad. Harshal Patel took 151 wickets at an economy of 8.92, the 12th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Harshal Patel scored 280 runs at a strike rate of 117.6 and an average of 8.75. Named Player of the Match five times.
