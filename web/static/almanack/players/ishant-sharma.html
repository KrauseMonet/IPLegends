The career

Ishant Sharma is a bowler from India who played 117 IPL matches in 15 seasons (2008 to 2025), for Kolkata Knight Riders, Deccan Chargers, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Rising Pune Supergiant, Punjab Kings, Delhi Capitals and Gujarat Titans. Ishant Sharma took 96 wickets at an economy of 8.38, the 34th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match four times.
