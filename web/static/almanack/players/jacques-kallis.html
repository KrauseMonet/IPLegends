The career

Jacques Kallis is an all-rounder from South Africa who played 98 IPL matches in 7 seasons (2008 to 2014), for Royal Challengers Bengaluru and Kolkata Knight Riders. Jacques Kallis scored 2,427 runs at a strike rate of 109.2 and an average of 28.55, the 47th-most runs in the IPL's history. Jacques Kallis took 65 wickets at an economy of 7.90. Named Player of the Match ten times.
