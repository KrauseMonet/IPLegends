The career

Kieron Pollard is an all-rounder from West Indies who played 189 IPL matches in 13 seasons (2010 to 2022), for Mumbai Indians. Kieron Pollard scored 3,412 runs at a strike rate of 147.3 and an average of 28.67, the 27th-most runs in the IPL's history. Kieron Pollard took 69 wickets at an economy of 8.79.
