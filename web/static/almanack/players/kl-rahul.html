The career

KL Rahul is a wicketkeeper-batter from India who played 160 IPL matches in 13 seasons (2013 to 2026), for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Punjab Kings, Lucknow Super Giants and Delhi Capitals. KL Rahul scored 5,815 runs at a strike rate of 139.1 and an average of 46.15, the 5th-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match 17 times.
