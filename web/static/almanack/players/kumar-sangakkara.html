The career

Kumar Sangakkara is a wicketkeeper-batter from Sri Lanka who played 71 IPL matches in 6 seasons (2008 to 2013), for Punjab Kings, Deccan Chargers and Sunrisers Hyderabad. Kumar Sangakkara scored 1,687 runs at a strike rate of 121.2 and an average of 25.95. Named Player of the Match five times.
