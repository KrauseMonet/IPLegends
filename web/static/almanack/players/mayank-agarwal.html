The career

Mayank Agarwal is a batter from India who played 131 IPL matches in 15 seasons (2011 to 2025), for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals, Rising Pune Supergiant, Punjab Kings and Sunrisers Hyderabad. Mayank Agarwal scored 2,756 runs at a strike rate of 133.5 and an average of 22.97, the 37th-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match four times.
