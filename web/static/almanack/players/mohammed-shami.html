The career

Mohammed Shami is a bowler from India who played 132 IPL matches in 12 seasons (2013 to 2026), for Kolkata Knight Riders, Delhi Capitals, Punjab Kings, Gujarat Titans, Sunrisers Hyderabad and Lucknow Super Giants. Mohammed Shami took 145 wickets at an economy of 8.67, the 17th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Mohammed Shami scored 115 runs at a strike rate of 103.6 and an average of 6.05. Named Player of the Match four times.
