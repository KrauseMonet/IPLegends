The career

Mohammed Siraj is a bowler from India who played 125 IPL matches in 10 seasons (2017 to 2026), for Sunrisers Hyderabad, Royal Challengers Bengaluru and Gujarat Titans. Mohammed Siraj took 128 wickets at an economy of 8.78, the 21st-most wickets in the IPL's history. Mohammed Siraj scored 117 runs at a strike rate of 84.2 and an average of 9.75.
