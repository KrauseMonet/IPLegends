The career

MS Dhoni is a wicketkeeper-batter from India who played 277 IPL matches in 18 seasons (2008 to 2025), for Chennai Super Kings and Rising Pune Supergiant. MS Dhoni scored 5,439 runs at a strike rate of 137.5 and an average of 38.30, the 7th-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match 18 times.
