The career

Murali Vijay is a batter from India who played 106 IPL matches in 11 seasons (2009 to 2020), for Chennai Super Kings, Delhi Capitals and Punjab Kings. Murali Vijay scored 2,619 runs at a strike rate of 121.9 and an average of 25.93, the 41st-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match six times.
