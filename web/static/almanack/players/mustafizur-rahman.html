The career

Mustafizur Rahman is a bowler from Bangladesh who played 60 IPL matches in 8 seasons (2016 to 2025), for Sunrisers Hyderabad, Mumbai Indians, Rajasthan Royals, Delhi Capitals and Chennai Super Kings. Mustafizur Rahman took 65 wickets at an economy of 8.13. Named Player of the Match twice.
