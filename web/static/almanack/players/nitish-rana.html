The career

Nitish Rana is a batter from India who played 127 IPL matches in 11 seasons (2016 to 2026), for Mumbai Indians, Kolkata Knight Riders, Rajasthan Royals and Delhi Capitals. Nitish Rana scored 3,078 runs at a strike rate of 137.7 and an average of 27.73, the 30th-most runs in the IPL's history. Nitish Rana took 10 wickets at an economy of 9.03. Named Player of the Match six times.
