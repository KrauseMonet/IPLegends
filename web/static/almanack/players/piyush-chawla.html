The career

Piyush Chawla is a bowler from India who played 192 IPL matches in 16 seasons (2008 to 2024), for Punjab Kings, Kolkata Knight Riders, Chennai Super Kings and Mumbai Indians. Piyush Chawla took 192 wickets at an economy of 7.96, the 4th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Piyush Chawla scored 624 runs at a strike rate of 110.8 and an average of 11.14. Named Player of the Match three times.
