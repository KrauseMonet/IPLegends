The career

Praveen Kumar is a bowler from India who played 119 IPL matches in 10 seasons (2008 to 2017), for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Punjab Kings, Mumbai Indians, Sunrisers Hyderabad and Gujarat Lions. Praveen Kumar took 90 wickets at an economy of 7.73, the 41st-most wickets in the IPL's history. Praveen Kumar scored 340 runs at a strike rate of 108.3 and an average of 8.95. Named Player of the Match twice.
