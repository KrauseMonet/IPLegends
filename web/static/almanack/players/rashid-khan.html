The career

Rashid Khan is a bowler from Afghanistan who played 153 IPL matches in 10 seasons (2017 to 2026), for Sunrisers Hyderabad and Gujarat Titans. Rashid Khan took 179 wickets at an economy of 7.28, the 9th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Rashid Khan scored 635 runs at a strike rate of 156.4 and an average of 13.80.
