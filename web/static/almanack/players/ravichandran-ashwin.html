The career

Ravichandran Ashwin is a bowler from India who played 219 IPL matches in 16 seasons (2009 to 2025), for Chennai Super Kings, Rising Pune Supergiant, Punjab Kings, Delhi Capitals and Rajasthan Royals. Ravichandran Ashwin took 187 wickets at an economy of 7.20, the 6th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Ravichandran Ashwin scored 833 runs at a strike rate of 118.2 and an average of 13.02. Named Player of the Match six times.
