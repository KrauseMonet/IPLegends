The career

Ravindra Jadeja is an all-rounder from India who played 268 IPL matches in 18 seasons (2008 to 2026), for Rajasthan Royals, Kochi Tuskers Kerala, Chennai Super Kings and Gujarat Lions. Ravindra Jadeja scored 3,526 runs at a strike rate of 130.6 and an average of 29.14, the 25th-most runs in the IPL's history. Ravindra Jadeja took 180 wickets at an economy of 7.71, the 8th-most wickets in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match 17 times.
