The career

Rishabh Pant is a wicketkeeper-batter from India who played 139 IPL matches in 10 seasons (2016 to 2026), for Delhi Capitals and Lucknow Super Giants. Rishabh Pant scored 3,865 runs at a strike rate of 146.8 and an average of 33.61, the 23rd-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match eight times.
