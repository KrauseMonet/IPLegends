The career

Sachin Tendulkar is a batter from India who played 78 IPL matches in 6 seasons (2008 to 2013), for Mumbai Indians. Sachin Tendulkar scored 2,334 runs at a strike rate of 119.8 and an average of 34.84, the 50th-most runs in the IPL's history. Named Player of the Match eight times.
