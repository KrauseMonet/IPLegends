The career

Yusuf Pathan is a batter from India who played 174 IPL matches in 12 seasons (2008 to 2019), for Rajasthan Royals, Kolkata Knight Riders and Sunrisers Hyderabad. Yusuf Pathan scored 3,204 runs at a strike rate of 143.0 and an average of 29.13, the 29th-most runs in the IPL's history. Yusuf Pathan took 42 wickets at an economy of 7.40. Named Player of the Match 16 times.
