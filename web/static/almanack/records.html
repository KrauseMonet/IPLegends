The record book

The IPL's record book from every ball since 2008: the highest scores and the fastest fifties, the best spells, the biggest partnerships, the highest totals and chases, and every hat-trick and super over. Every figure is worked out from the ball-by-ball archive, in ball order, so a fastest fifty is the ball the fiftieth run came off, not an estimate from a scorecard. Matches cut short by rain are left out of the team totals.
