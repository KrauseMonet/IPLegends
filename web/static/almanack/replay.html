Match replays

Watch it again

Every one of the 1,243 IPL matches in the archive, played again over by over on the same scoreboard the game uses: the score, who is at the crease, each over's runs and wickets, and every fifty, hundred and five-for as it came. Then ask what if. Take any player out of either side and bring in any player-season from nineteen years of the IPL, and the match engine plays it again. It also plays each match a hundred times to say how often each side wins, so a result can be read as the expected one or an upset.
