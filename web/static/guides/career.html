Build a Career

The idea

Build a Career puts you in the boots of one player, from a 19-year-old debutant to the end of his time in the game. You choose who he is and what he does. Then, season by season, he plays through the real history of the IPL: the real teams of each year, real teammates and real opponents, in matches played ball by ball by the same engine as the rest of the site. Your choices shape the player; the matches decide how far he goes.

Creating your player

You pick a name, a debut year between 2008 and 2017, whether you are an Indian or an overseas player, and one of seven roles: opening batter, middle-order batter, finisher, wicketkeeper-batter, fast bowler, spinner or all-rounder. You can also give him a shirt number, a home town, a celebration and up to three ambitions to chase, such as winning the title or playing your whole career for one club.

Two spins then set his range. The first draws a real player's real season as your starting level, and the second draws a better real season as your ceiling, the most you can grow into. Every number your player plays with is therefore a real player's number, not an invented one. Each career also has a hidden shape: some players peak early, some late, and some decline gently while others fade fast. You find out which kind you are as the seasons pass.

Each season

A season starts with offers. Clubs that want you say what they will pay and where you would play: which batting position, or where in the bowling attack. You can sign for one year, or take a three-year contract at a lower price for security. A contract protects you from being released for poor form while it runs.

Then comes a pre-season moment: a coach who wants to change your technique, a senior player offering to mentor you, the captaincy, a sponsor, a rivalry, a trade the club has agreed. Each answer has a consequence, whether for your form, your standing at the club, or the kind of player you become.

The season is then played match by match. You see each result, your own figures, the league table and the Orange and Purple Cap race as it unfolds. In matches that matter, such as a must-win league game or any playoff, the game stops and asks how you want to play it: carefully, normally or all-out attack. In the middle of a season things happen too: an injury, a slump, a row in the dressing room, a call to bowl the death overs. A run of poor scores can put you in a make-or-break match, and failing it costs you your place for two games.

Rating and season grade

Your player has two numbers. His Rating is his underlying ability, which rises as he develops and falls as he ages. His season grade is how well each season actually went, compared with the real players of his kind in that same season. Good and bad seasons happen to every player, so a season grade can sit well above or below the Rating; what you do in good years, and how you handle the bad ones, is the story of the career.

Strong seasons while you are still developing add to your growth, and a season far above your expected level can even raise your ceiling. Poor seasons in a row make you a squad player, and eventually nobody signs you. Late in a career, good form slows the decline and poor form speeds it up.

Auctions, retention and your country

In the real mega-auction years, 2011, 2014, 2018, 2022 and 2025, every contract ends. If you have been good enough, your club offers to retain you at a fixed price. You can accept, or turn it down and go into the auction at a base price you choose, gambling on a bigger fee or a better club. The bidding is shown as it happens.

Good seasons can also earn a call-up to your national side. The chances are based on how often real IPL players with similar seasons were picked, and a capped international is worth more to the clubs.

The end of the story

A career ends when nobody will sign you, when you choose to retire, or when the archive runs out in 2026. The career card then sums it all up: runs, wickets, titles, caps, earnings, your Rating season by season, every milestone and where you would rank among the real all-time leaders. Newspaper clippings from The Fine Leg Gazette, a made-up paper, tell the big moments along the way. If you are signed in, finished careers are saved to a Hall of Fame on your profile, and the best season of a saved career can be brought into an auction with friends as a free extra player.
