Draft and season

The idea

The draft asks one question: if you could pick any players from any IPL season, could you build a side that wins a title? You pick twelve cards one at a time, each card being one player's real season, and then play a full IPL season with them against nine sides drawn from the real history of the competition. Nothing needs installing and no account is required. A draft takes about five minutes; a season can be played in a minute or watched over by over.

How a pick works

Each pick deals you one real franchise-season, for example Mumbai Indians 2013 or Rajasthan Royals 2008, chosen at random from the 166 squads in the archive. Every season has the same chance of being dealt, so Mumbai, who have played nineteen seasons, turn up far more often than Kochi Tuskers Kerala, who played one. A rare squad is meant to feel like an event.

The whole squad is shown. Players you cannot take are greyed out with the reason beside them: already in your side, no batting place open that suits them, or your overseas places are full. You pick one player and the place he will fill in the same move: a batting position from one to eleven, or the Impact Player. A placement is final. You can move players you have already picked around the order to make room, but nobody leaves the side once picked.

You have one reroll for the whole draft. Spend it to see a different squad for the same pick, either another franchise or another season of the franchise just dealt.

The rules of a legal twelve

  • Eleven batting places and an Impact Player. The Impact Player follows the rule the IPL introduced in 2023: he can come in at the innings break to strengthen whichever side of the game your team still has to play.
  • One wicketkeeper at least. Any player who kept wicket in an IPL season, or who is a wicketkeeper by trade, can fill the role.
  • Five bowling options. Twenty overs at four overs a bowler needs five bowlers.
  • No more than four overseas players in the twelve. Because the squad itself is capped, every eleven you can field from it is legal.
  • Batting positions follow real careers. Each player has a batting role, read from where he actually batted: top order (positions one to three), middle order (three to five), finishers (five to seven) and tail (eight to eleven). You cannot open with a number nine. Where a player batted in different roles in different seasons, the role of that season is used.

The game checks after every pick that your side can still be completed, so you can never be left needing a keeper with no way to get one. If a deal would leave you stuck, a different squad is dealt instead.

Stat mode and memory mode

In stat mode every card shows its rating and the player's real figures for that season. In memory mode the numbers are hidden and you pick on what you remember about the players, which is harder and, for many people, more fun. The ratings are revealed once your twelve is complete. How the ratings are worked out is explained in how the ratings work.

The season

Your side joins nine real franchise-seasons, each fielding the best legal eleven its real squad could put out. Each team plays fourteen league matches. Two points for a win, and sides level on points are separated by net run rate, where a side bowled out is charged the full twenty overs, as in the real competition.

The top four go into the IPL's own playoff format, not a simple bracket. First plays second in Qualifier 1, and the winner goes straight to the final. Third plays fourth in the Eliminator, and the loser goes home. The loser of Qualifier 1 gets a second chance against the winner of the Eliminator in Qualifier 2. Finishing in the top two is therefore worth a great deal.

A tied match goes to a super over, as every tied IPL match now does. Super over runs count only for the result, never for net run rate or the batting and bowling figures.

Playing or watching

When the draft is done you choose how to see the season: play the whole tournament at once, go match by match, or play the group stage and then watch the playoffs. In a match you play, you call the toss and choose to bat or bowl, and at the innings break you decide whether to bring in your Impact Player and whom he replaces. The game suggests an Impact Player change, and you can accept it or overrule it. Matches can be watched over by over with a live scoreboard, the run rate, the chase equation and the milestones as they happen: fifties, hundreds, five-wicket hauls and hat-tricks.

Afterwards the season page shows the full points table, the playoff bracket, your journey card with your top scorer and wicket-taker, the Orange and Purple Caps and a Season Analysis screen with a Manhattan chart and phase-by-phase figures.

Advice for a better twelve

  • Fill the scarce places early. Good keepers and specialist death bowlers are rarer than good openers. If a strong keeper is dealt early, take him.
  • Count your bowling. A sixth bowling option gives cover for an expensive day, and an all-rounder who bowls four overs frees a batting place.
  • Use the overseas places wisely. Four is the limit, and many of the highest-rated seasons belong to overseas players. Spending them all in the first four picks leaves little room later.
  • Think about the Impact Player. A specialist bowler or a specialist batter is the best use of the twelfth place, because only one half of his game will be used.
  • The order matters. Positions decide how many balls each batter faces. Put your best batters at the top, where they will face the most.

With friends, and the daily challenge

In Rooms you draft against friends in real time from one shared pool, so a player one of you takes is gone for the others. A room can be a one-match final for two, a four-team cup, or a full ten-team league with the IPL playoffs. Empty places are filled by real historical squads. Each pick has a timer; if it runs out, a legal player is picked for you at random.

The daily challenge gives everybody the same sixteen squads and the same target for the day, such as winning by more than twenty runs against a named historical side. There are no rerolls and one attempt, and the result goes on a leaderboard.
