Nineteen seasons · one eleven

Every season is a hand.
Play the best eleven ever dealt.

You are dealt one franchise-season at a time — Chennai 2010, Kolkata 2008, Rajasthan 2022 — and you may take one player from it, for a place you still need. Fifteen selections, no more than four overseas. Then your eleven takes the field for twenty overs against the house.

Field placings · illustrative

I. The deal

A franchise-season drawn evenly from all one hundred and sixty-six. Mumbai answers nineteen times; Kochi Tuskers Kerala, once only.

II. The selection

One man from that squad, for a place still open. He must have played that season — there are no imaginary teams here.

III. The match

Twenty overs a side, delivery by delivery, priced from every ball bowled in the competition since 2008.
