The IPL 2027 auction

16 December 2026

The IPL 2027 auction is a mini auction: the ten franchises kept most of their squads, and go in with ₹297.95 cr between them for 66 places, 25 of them for overseas players. Every squad must reach at least 18 players and may hold 25, no more than eight of them from overseas. Lucknow Super Giants have the most to spend, ₹47.45 cr; Gujarat Titans the least, ₹8.75 cr for 3 places.
