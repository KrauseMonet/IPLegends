What the pass includes

  • Career Pro — up to five careers kept on your account and playable on any device, and any of your league matches watched ball by ball (playoffs stay free).
  • Career boosts — play a career boosted: a second ceiling spin (the better is kept), one release survived, and an extra step each time you meet your focus.
  • Club dynasty — one club whose careers you build into a story, with your retired players coaching, running the academy and commentating for the next.
  • Pro rooms — make a friends' room Pro and everyone in it gets a second career legend, three trades instead of two, and Run it back: a fresh tournament with the same squads. Only the host needs a pass.
  • Pass perks in rooms — one extra career legend, and legends from boosted careers. Marked ★ for everyone in the room, and the host can switch them off.
  • Pass colours for your team kit, the Pass edition career card, and a ★ beside your name.

Planned, and covered by a pass when they arrive: what-if seasons, rooms with your own rules and a league table across them, and every past daily puzzle.

Never on sale: anything on the daily challenge or puzzle leaderboards. No prizes, no packs.

How paying works

  1. Choose a pass. You get a six-letter reference.
  2. Pay by UPI with that reference in the note.
  3. Paste the 12-digit UTR (UPI transaction ID) from your UPI app here.
  4. The payment is checked by hand, usually within a day, and your pass starts.

Paid but your pass hasn't started after two days? Write to us from the contact page with your reference. Refunds are covered in the terms.
