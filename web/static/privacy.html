If you don't create an account

Drafting, playing a solo season, and playing in a room all work without signing in, and without anything about you being stored beyond what any web server ordinarily logs to run and keep the site working (request logs held by the hosting provider). A room seat you join anonymously is identified only by a random ID kept in your own browser's local storage, never sent anywhere else and never linked to a real identity. The daily challenge works the same way when you play it signed out: a random ID in your browser decides your deal, your result is worked out and shown to you, and nothing about it is stored.

If you create an account

Registering stores a username, an email address, and a password. The password is never stored as typed — it's run through a standard one-way hashing algorithm before it's saved, so even we can't read it back. Signing in sets one cookie that identifies your session; it carries no tracking purpose and isn't shared with anyone.

If you play the daily challenge while signed in, your result goes on that day's public leaderboard under your username, which anyone visiting the site can see. Your email address is never shown.

If you save a game while signed in

Saving is something you (or the site, on your behalf, once you've completed a game while signed in) trigger explicitly — it never happens silently. What's stored is the squad you played with and how each of those real players performed in that one simulated game (runs, wickets), linked to your account so your profile can total them up. Nothing is stored about a game you play anonymously, and nothing is backfilled after the fact.

Cookies

The site sets exactly one cookie, for staying signed in. There are no ads, and no analytics or tracking cookies, on any page.

If you send a tip

Tipping is by UPI, and it happens entirely inside your own UPI app — the QR and the link simply hand your app a payment address. No payment page is hosted here, no card or bank details are ever entered on this site, and nothing about a tip is recorded against your account or anything else. Whatever your bank or UPI app shows the recipient is between you and them, under their own policies rather than this one.

Who this is shared with

Nobody buys or sells anything here. Data is held with the infrastructure providers needed to run the site — a hosting platform and a database provider — and, for ad delivery on the home page, Google. It isn't shared for any other purpose.

Deleting your data

There's no self-service deletion yet. Email mishrakoustav01@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask, and it'll be removed — the account, its saved games, and everything linked to it.

Children

This site isn't directed at, and knowingly doesn't collect information from, children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will move to reflect it.
