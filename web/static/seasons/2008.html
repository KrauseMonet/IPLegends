IPL HISTORY

IPL 2008

The season

The 2008 Indian Premier League was the 1st season of the competition. Eight teams played the 58 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 18 April 2008 and 1 June 2008, at 9 grounds in 8 cities; M Chinnaswamy Stadium staged the most, 7.

Rajasthan Royals won their first title, beating Chennai Super Kings by 3 wickets in the final at Dr DY Patil Sports Academy on 1 June 2008. The final finished Chennai Super Kings 163/5 (20 ov) · Rajasthan Royals 164/7 (20 ov). Yusuf Pathan was named Player of the Match.

Shaun Marsh (Kings XI Punjab) scored the most runs, 616 at a strike rate of 139.7, including a hundred. Sohail Tanvir (Rajasthan Royals) took the most wickets, 22 at an economy rate of 6.46.

Rajasthan Royals topped the league stage with 22 points; Rajasthan Royals, Kings XI Punjab, Chennai Super Kings and Delhi Daredevils were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Chennai Super Kings' 240/5 against Kings XI Punjab, and the lowest all-out total Kolkata Knight Riders' 67. Batters hit 623 sixes and 1,703 fours, and there were 6 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 161.

The eight founding sides were Chennai Super Kings, Deccan Chargers, Delhi Daredevils, Kings XI Punjab, Kolkata Knight Riders, Mumbai Indians, Rajasthan Royals and Royal Challengers Bangalore.
