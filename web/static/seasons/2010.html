IPL 2010

The season

The 2010 Indian Premier League was the 3rd season of the competition. Eight teams played the 60 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 12 March 2010 and 25 April 2010, at 13 grounds in 11 cities; Eden Gardens staged the most, 7.

Chennai Super Kings won their first title, beating Mumbai Indians by 22 runs in the final at Dr DY Patil Sports Academy on 25 April 2010. The final finished Chennai Super Kings 168/5 (20 ov) · Mumbai Indians 146/9 (20 ov). Suresh Raina was named Player of the Match.

Sachin Tendulkar (Mumbai Indians) scored the most runs, 618 at a strike rate of 132.6. Pragyan Ojha (Deccan Chargers) took the most wickets, 21 at an economy rate of 7.29.

Mumbai Indians topped the league stage with 20 points; Mumbai Indians, Deccan Chargers, Chennai Super Kings and Royal Challengers Bangalore were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Chennai Super Kings' 246/5 against Rajasthan Royals, and the lowest all-out total Deccan Chargers' 82. Batters hit 585 sixes and 1,708 fours, and there were 4 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 165.
