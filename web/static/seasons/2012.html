IPL 2012

The season

The 2012 Indian Premier League was the 5th season of the competition. Nine teams played the 74 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 4 April 2012 and 27 May 2012, at 12 grounds in 12 cities; MA Chidambaram Stadium, Chepauk staged the most, 10.

Kolkata Knight Riders won their first title, beating Chennai Super Kings by 5 wickets in the final at MA Chidambaram Stadium, Chepauk on 27 May 2012. The final finished Chennai Super Kings 190/3 (20 ov) · Kolkata Knight Riders 192/5 (19.4 ov). Manvinder Bisla was named Player of the Match.

Chris Gayle (Royal Challengers Bangalore) scored the most runs, 733 at a strike rate of 160.7, including a hundred. Morné Morkel (Delhi Daredevils) took the most wickets, 25 at an economy rate of 7.19.

Delhi Daredevils topped the league stage with 22 points; Delhi Daredevils, Kolkata Knight Riders, Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Chennai Super Kings' 222/5 against Delhi Daredevils, and the lowest all-out total Mumbai Indians' 92. Batters hit 733 sixes and 1,911 fours, and there were 6 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 158.

Kochi Tuskers Kerala did not take part.
