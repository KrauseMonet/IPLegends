IPL 2013

The season

The 2013 Indian Premier League was the 6th season of the competition. Nine teams played the 76 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 3 April 2013 and 26 May 2013, at 13 grounds in 12 cities; Eden Gardens staged the most, 8.

Mumbai Indians won their first title, beating Chennai Super Kings by 23 runs in the final at Eden Gardens on 26 May 2013. The final finished Mumbai Indians 148/9 (20 ov) · Chennai Super Kings 125/9 (20 ov). Kieron Pollard was named Player of the Match.

Michael Hussey (Chennai Super Kings) scored the most runs, 733 at a strike rate of 129.5. Dwayne Bravo (Chennai Super Kings) took the most wickets, 32 at an economy rate of 7.95.

Chennai Super Kings and Mumbai Indians finished level at the top of the league stage on 22 points; Chennai Super Kings, Mumbai Indians, Rajasthan Royals and Sunrisers Hyderabad were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Royal Challengers Bangalore's 263/5 against Pune Warriors, and the lowest all-out total Chennai Super Kings' 79. Batters hit 675 sixes and 2,052 fours, and there were 4 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 156.

Sunrisers Hyderabad played its first season. Deccan Chargers did not take part.
