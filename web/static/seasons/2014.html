IPL 2014

The season

The 2014 Indian Premier League was the 7th season of the competition. Eight teams played the 60 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 16 April 2014 and 1 June 2014, at 13 grounds in 10 cities; Sheikh Zayed Stadium staged the most, 7.

Kolkata Knight Riders won their second title, beating Kings XI Punjab by 3 wickets in the final at M Chinnaswamy Stadium on 1 June 2014. The final finished Kings XI Punjab 199/4 (20 ov) · Kolkata Knight Riders 200/7 (19.3 ov). Manish Pandey was named Player of the Match.

Robin Uthappa (Kolkata Knight Riders) scored the most runs, 660 at a strike rate of 137.8. Mohit Sharma (Chennai Super Kings) took the most wickets, 23 at an economy rate of 8.40.

Kings XI Punjab topped the league stage with 22 points; Kings XI Punjab, Chennai Super Kings, Kolkata Knight Riders and Mumbai Indians were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Kings XI Punjab's 231/4 against Chennai Super Kings, and the lowest all-out total Royal Challengers Bangalore's 70. Batters hit 714 sixes and 1,562 fours, and there were 3 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 163.

Pune Warriors did not take part.
