IPL 2015

The season

The 2015 Indian Premier League was the 8th season of the competition. Eight teams played the 59 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 8 April 2015 and 24 May 2015, at 13 grounds in 12 cities; Wankhede Stadium staged the most, 8.

Mumbai Indians won their second title, beating Chennai Super Kings by 41 runs in the final at Eden Gardens on 24 May 2015. The final finished Mumbai Indians 202/5 (20 ov) · Chennai Super Kings 161/8 (20 ov). Rohit Sharma was named Player of the Match.

David Warner (Sunrisers Hyderabad) scored the most runs, 562 at a strike rate of 156.5. Dwayne Bravo (Chennai Super Kings) took the most wickets, 26 at an economy rate of 8.14.

Chennai Super Kings topped the league stage with 18 points; Chennai Super Kings, Mumbai Indians, Royal Challengers Bangalore and Rajasthan Royals were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Royal Challengers Bangalore's 235/1 against Mumbai Indians, and the lowest all-out total Kings XI Punjab's 88. Batters hit 692 sixes and 1,607 fours, and there were 4 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 166.
