IPL HISTORY

IPL 2016

The season

The 2016 Indian Premier League was the 9th season of the competition. Eight teams played the 60 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 9 April 2016 and 29 May 2016, at 11 grounds in 11 cities; M Chinnaswamy Stadium staged the most, 9.

Sunrisers Hyderabad won their first title, beating Royal Challengers Bangalore by 8 runs in the final at M Chinnaswamy Stadium on 29 May 2016. The final finished Sunrisers Hyderabad 208/7 (20 ov) · Royal Challengers Bangalore 200/7 (20 ov). Ben Cutting was named Player of the Match.

Virat Kohli (Royal Challengers Bangalore) scored the most runs, 973 at a strike rate of 152.0, including 4 hundreds. Bhuvneshwar Kumar (Sunrisers Hyderabad) took the most wickets, 23 at an economy rate of 7.42.

Gujarat Lions topped the league stage with 18 points; Gujarat Lions, Kolkata Knight Riders, Royal Challengers Bangalore and Sunrisers Hyderabad were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Royal Challengers Bangalore's 248/3 against Gujarat Lions, and the lowest all-out total Mumbai Indians' 92. Batters hit 639 sixes and 1,633 fours, and there were 7 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 163.

Gujarat Lions and Rising Pune Supergiants played their first season. Chennai Super Kings and Rajasthan Royals did not take part.
