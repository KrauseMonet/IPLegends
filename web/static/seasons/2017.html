IPL 2017

The season

The 2017 Indian Premier League was the 10th season of the competition. Eight teams played the 59 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 5 April 2017 and 21 May 2017, at 11 grounds in 11 cities; Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium, Uppal staged the most, 8.

Mumbai Indians won their third title, beating Rising Pune Supergiant by 1 run in the final at Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium, Uppal on 21 May 2017. The final finished Mumbai Indians 129/8 (20 ov) · Rising Pune Supergiant 128/6 (20 ov). Krunal Pandya was named Player of the Match.

David Warner (Sunrisers Hyderabad) scored the most runs, 641 at a strike rate of 141.8, including a hundred. Bhuvneshwar Kumar (Sunrisers Hyderabad) took the most wickets, 26 at an economy rate of 7.05.

Mumbai Indians topped the league stage with 20 points; Mumbai Indians, Rising Pune Supergiant, Kolkata Knight Riders and Sunrisers Hyderabad were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Kings XI Punjab's 230/3 against Mumbai Indians, and the lowest all-out total Royal Challengers Bangalore's 49. Batters hit 705 sixes and 1,611 fours, and there were 5 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 166.

Rising Pune Supergiants became Rising Pune Supergiant.
