IPL 2018

The season

The 2018 Indian Premier League was the 11th season of the competition. Eight teams played the 60 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 7 April 2018 and 27 May 2018, at 10 grounds in 10 cities; Wankhede Stadium staged the most, 9.

Chennai Super Kings won their third title, beating Sunrisers Hyderabad by 8 wickets in the final at Wankhede Stadium on 27 May 2018. The final finished Sunrisers Hyderabad 178/6 (20 ov) · Chennai Super Kings 181/2 (18.3 ov). Shane Watson was named Player of the Match.

Kane Williamson (Sunrisers Hyderabad) scored the most runs, 735 at a strike rate of 142.4. Andrew Tye (Kings XI Punjab) took the most wickets, 24 at an economy rate of 8.00.

Chennai Super Kings and Sunrisers Hyderabad finished level at the top of the league stage on 18 points; Chennai Super Kings, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Kolkata Knight Riders and Rajasthan Royals were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Kolkata Knight Riders' 245/6 against Kings XI Punjab, and the lowest all-out total Mumbai Indians' 87. Batters hit 872 sixes and 1,652 fours, and there were 5 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 172.

Chennai Super Kings and Rajasthan Royals returned after time away. Gujarat Lions and Rising Pune Supergiant did not take part.
