IPL 2019

The season

The 2019 Indian Premier League was the 12th season of the competition. Eight teams played the 60 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 23 March 2019 and 12 May 2019, at 9 grounds in 9 cities; MA Chidambaram Stadium staged the most, 8.

Mumbai Indians won their fourth title, beating Chennai Super Kings by 1 run in the final at Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium on 12 May 2019. The final finished Mumbai Indians 149/8 (20 ov) · Chennai Super Kings 148/7 (20 ov). Jasprit Bumrah was named Player of the Match.

David Warner (Sunrisers Hyderabad) scored the most runs, 692 at a strike rate of 143.9, including a hundred. Imran Tahir (Chennai Super Kings) took the most wickets, 26 at an economy rate of 6.70.

Chennai Super Kings, Delhi Capitals and Mumbai Indians finished level at the top of the league stage on 18 points; Chennai Super Kings, Delhi Capitals, Mumbai Indians and Sunrisers Hyderabad were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Kolkata Knight Riders' 232/2 against Mumbai Indians, and the lowest all-out total Royal Challengers Bangalore's 70. Batters hit 784 sixes and 1,653 fours, and there were 6 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 167.

Delhi Daredevils became Delhi Capitals.
