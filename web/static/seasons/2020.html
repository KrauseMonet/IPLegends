IPL 2020

The season

The 2020 Indian Premier League was the 13th season of the competition. Eight teams played the 60 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 19 September 2020 and 10 November 2020, at 3 grounds in 1 city; Dubai International Cricket Stadium staged the most, 26.

Mumbai Indians won their fifth title, beating Delhi Capitals by 5 wickets in the final at Dubai International Cricket Stadium on 10 November 2020. The final finished Delhi Capitals 156/7 (20 ov) · Mumbai Indians 157/5 (18.4 ov). Trent Boult was named Player of the Match.

KL Rahul (Kings XI Punjab) scored the most runs, 670 at a strike rate of 129.3, including a hundred. Kagiso Rabada (Delhi Capitals) took the most wickets, 30 at an economy rate of 8.35.

Mumbai Indians topped the league stage with 18 points; Mumbai Indians, Delhi Capitals, Royal Challengers Bangalore and Sunrisers Hyderabad were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Delhi Capitals' 228/4 against Kolkata Knight Riders, and the lowest all-out total Royal Challengers Bangalore's 109. Batters hit 735 sixes and 1,583 fours, and there were 5 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 170.
