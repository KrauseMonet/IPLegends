IPL 2021

The season

The 2021 Indian Premier League was the 14th season of the competition. Eight teams played the 60 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 9 April 2021 and 15 October 2021, at 7 grounds in 7 cities; Dubai International Cricket Stadium staged the most, 13.

Chennai Super Kings won their fourth title, beating Kolkata Knight Riders by 27 runs in the final at Dubai International Cricket Stadium on 15 October 2021. The final finished Chennai Super Kings 192/3 (20 ov) · Kolkata Knight Riders 165/9 (20 ov). Faf du Plessis was named Player of the Match.

Ruturaj Gaikwad (Chennai Super Kings) scored the most runs, 635 at a strike rate of 136.3, including a hundred. Harshal Patel (Royal Challengers Bangalore) took the most wickets, 32 at an economy rate of 8.15.

Delhi Capitals topped the league stage with 20 points; Delhi Capitals, Chennai Super Kings, Royal Challengers Bangalore and Kolkata Knight Riders were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Mumbai Indians' 235/9 against Sunrisers Hyderabad, and the lowest all-out total Rajasthan Royals' 85. Batters hit 687 sixes and 1,549 fours, and there were 4 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 159.

Kings XI Punjab became Punjab Kings.
