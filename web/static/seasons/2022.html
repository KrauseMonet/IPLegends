IPL 2022

The season

The 2022 Indian Premier League was the 15th season of the competition. Ten teams played the 74 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 26 March 2022 and 29 May 2022, at 6 grounds in 5 cities; Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai staged the most, 21.

Gujarat Titans won their first title, beating Rajasthan Royals by 7 wickets in the final at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad on 29 May 2022. The final finished Rajasthan Royals 130/9 (20 ov) · Gujarat Titans 133/3 (18.1 ov). Hardik Pandya was named Player of the Match.

Jos Buttler (Rajasthan Royals) scored the most runs, 863 at a strike rate of 149.1, including 4 hundreds. Yuzvendra Chahal (Rajasthan Royals) took the most wickets, 27 at an economy rate of 7.75.

Gujarat Titans topped the league stage with 20 points; Gujarat Titans, Lucknow Super Giants, Rajasthan Royals and Royal Challengers Bangalore were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Rajasthan Royals' 222/2 against Delhi Capitals, and the lowest all-out total Royal Challengers Bangalore's 68. Batters hit 1,062 sixes and 2,020 fours, and there were 8 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 171.

Gujarat Titans and Lucknow Super Giants played their first season.
