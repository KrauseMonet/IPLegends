IPL 2023

The season

The 2023 Indian Premier League was the 16th season of the competition. Ten teams played the 74 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 31 March 2023 and 29 May 2023, at 12 grounds in 12 cities; Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad staged the most, 9.

Chennai Super Kings won their fifth title, beating Gujarat Titans by 5 wickets (DLS method) in the final at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad on 29 May 2023. The final finished Gujarat Titans 214/4 (20 ov) · Chennai Super Kings 171/5 (15 ov). Devon Conway was named Player of the Match.

Shubman Gill (Gujarat Titans) scored the most runs, 890 at a strike rate of 157.8, including 3 hundreds. Mohammed Shami (Gujarat Titans) took the most wickets, 28 at an economy rate of 8.03.

Gujarat Titans topped the league stage with 20 points; Gujarat Titans, Chennai Super Kings, Lucknow Super Giants and Mumbai Indians were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Lucknow Super Giants' 257/5 against Punjab Kings, and the lowest all-out total Rajasthan Royals' 59. Batters hit 1,124 sixes and 2,175 fours, and there were 12 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 183.
