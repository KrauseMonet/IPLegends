IPL 2024

The season

The 2024 Indian Premier League was the 17th season of the competition. Ten teams played the 71 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 22 March 2024 and 26 May 2024, at 13 grounds in 13 cities; MA Chidambaram Stadium, Chepauk, Chennai staged the most, 9.

Kolkata Knight Riders won their third title, beating Sunrisers Hyderabad by 8 wickets in the final at MA Chidambaram Stadium, Chepauk, Chennai on 26 May 2024. The final finished Sunrisers Hyderabad 113 (18.3 ov) · Kolkata Knight Riders 114/2 (10.3 ov). Mitchell Starc was named Player of the Match.

Virat Kohli (Royal Challengers Bengaluru) scored the most runs, 741 at a strike rate of 154.7, including a hundred. Harshal Patel (Punjab Kings) took the most wickets, 24 at an economy rate of 9.73.

Kolkata Knight Riders topped the league stage with 18 points; Kolkata Knight Riders, Rajasthan Royals, Sunrisers Hyderabad and Royal Challengers Bengaluru were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Sunrisers Hyderabad's 287/3 against Royal Challengers Bengaluru, and the lowest all-out total Gujarat Titans' 89. Batters hit 1,261 sixes and 2,175 fours, and there were 14 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 190.

Royal Challengers Bangalore became Royal Challengers Bengaluru.
