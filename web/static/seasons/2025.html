IPL 2025

The season

The 2025 Indian Premier League was the 18th season of the competition. Ten teams played the 74 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 22 March 2025 and 3 June 2025, at 14 grounds in 14 cities; Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad staged the most, 9.

Royal Challengers Bengaluru won their first title, beating Punjab Kings by 6 runs in the final at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad on 3 June 2025. The final finished Royal Challengers Bengaluru 190/9 (20 ov) · Punjab Kings 184/7 (20 ov). Krunal Pandya was named Player of the Match.

Sai Sudharsan (Gujarat Titans) scored the most runs, 759 at a strike rate of 156.2, including a hundred. Prasidh Krishna (Gujarat Titans) took the most wickets, 25 at an economy rate of 8.27.

Punjab Kings topped the league stage with 20 points; Punjab Kings, Gujarat Titans, Royal Challengers Bengaluru and Mumbai Indians were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Sunrisers Hyderabad's 286/6 against Rajasthan Royals, and the lowest all-out total Kolkata Knight Riders' 95. Batters hit 1,301 sixes and 2,260 fours, and there were 9 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 189.
