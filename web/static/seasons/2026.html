IPL 2026

The season

The 2026 Indian Premier League was the 19th season of the competition. Ten teams played the 74 matches in the ball-by-ball record between 28 March 2026 and 31 May 2026, at 13 grounds in 13 cities; Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad staged the most, 8.

Royal Challengers Bengaluru won their second title, beating Gujarat Titans by 5 wickets in the final at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad on 31 May 2026. The final finished Gujarat Titans 155/8 (20 ov) · Royal Challengers Bengaluru 161/5 (18 ov). Virat Kohli was named Player of the Match.

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi (Rajasthan Royals) scored the most runs, 776 at a strike rate of 237.3, including a hundred. Kagiso Rabada (Gujarat Titans) took the most wickets, 29 at an economy rate of 9.68.

Gujarat Titans, Royal Challengers Bengaluru and Sunrisers Hyderabad finished level at the top of the league stage on 18 points; Gujarat Titans, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Sunrisers Hyderabad and Rajasthan Royals were the four sides in the knockouts. The highest total of the season was Punjab Kings' 265/4 against Delhi Capitals, and the lowest all-out total Delhi Capitals' 75. Batters hit 1,426 sixes and 2,333 fours, and there were 15 hundreds in all; the average first-innings score was 193.
