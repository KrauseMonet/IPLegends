Fifteen franchises

15 franchises have played in the IPL since 2008. Some have changed their names, some have changed their crests, and several no longer play; each is counted here as one club across every name it has used, so Delhi Daredevils and Delhi Capitals are the same franchise, as are Kings XI Punjab and Punjab Kings. Each franchise page has its full season-by-season record, its titles and finals, and its leading run-scorers and wicket-takers.

Who has done best

Mumbai Indians have played the most matches in the ball-by-ball record, 291. Among franchises with at least a hundred matches, Chennai Super Kings have the best record, winning 56% of the matches that had a result, and Delhi Capitals the weakest, 47%. Chennai Super Kings have played in the most finals, 10. A title is not the whole story: several sides have topped the league stage and then lost in the knockouts, which is why each franchise page lists every season's finish as well as its trophies.

Names and changes

Ten franchises play today: Chennai Super Kings, Delhi Capitals, Gujarat Titans, Kolkata Knight Riders, Lucknow Super Giants, Mumbai Indians, Punjab Kings, Rajasthan Royals, Royal Challengers Bengaluru and Sunrisers Hyderabad. Over the years Delhi Daredevils became Delhi Capitals in 2019, Kings XI Punjab became Punjab Kings in 2021, Rising Pune Supergiants became Rising Pune Supergiant in 2017 and Royal Challengers Bangalore became Royal Challengers Bengaluru in 2024. Five franchises no longer play: Deccan Chargers (2008 to 2012), Gujarat Lions (2016 to 2017), Kochi Tuskers Kerala (2011), Pune Warriors (2011 to 2013) and Rising Pune Supergiant (2016 to 2017). A franchise's crest on these pages is the one it wore in that season, so the old Delhi Daredevils and Kings XI Punjab crests appear in the years they were used.
