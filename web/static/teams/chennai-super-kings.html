Chennai Super Kings

The franchise

Chennai Super Kings have played 17 seasons of the IPL, from 2008 to 2026. They did not take part in 2016 and 2017. Across 266 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 148 and lost 117, with 1 no result, winning 56% of the matches that had a result; one of those wins and losses came in a super over after a tie.

They have won the title five times (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021 and 2023) and lost five finals (2008, 2012, 2013, 2015 and 2019). They reached the knockouts in 12 seasons of 17. Their most wins in a season came in 2013, 12 from 18 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is MS Dhoni, with 4,865 runs in 16 seasons at a strike rate of 139.1. Ravindra Jadeja has taken the most wickets for them, 143. The highest score made for the franchise is Murali Vijay's 127 against Rajasthan Royals in 2010, and their highest total 246/5 against Rajasthan Royals in 2010. They have played more matches in Chennai than anywhere else, 84.
