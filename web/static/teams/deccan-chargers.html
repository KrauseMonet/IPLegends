Deccan Chargers

The franchise

Deccan Chargers played 5 seasons of the IPL, from 2008 to 2012. The franchise no longer plays. Across 75 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 29 and lost 46, winning 39% of the matches that had a result.

They won the title once (2009). They reached the knockouts in 2 seasons of 5. Their most wins in a season came in 2009, 9 from 16 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is Adam Gilchrist, with 1,220 runs in 3 seasons at a strike rate of 147.3. Pragyan Ojha took the most wickets for them, 60. The highest score made for the franchise is Andrew Symonds' 117* against Rajasthan Royals in 2008, and their highest total 214/5 against Rajasthan Royals in 2008. They played more matches in Hyderabad than anywhere else, 18.
