Delhi Capitals

The franchise

Delhi Capitals have played 19 seasons of the IPL, from 2008 to 2026. The franchise has played as Delhi Daredevils (2008–2018) and Delhi Capitals (2019–2026). Across 281 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 129 and lost 148, with 4 no results, winning 47% of the matches that had a result; five of those wins and losses came in a super over after a tie.

They have never won the title, and have been runners-up once (2020)). They reached the knockouts in 6 seasons of 19. Their most wins in a season came in 2012, 11 from 18 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is Rishabh Pant, with 3,284 runs in 8 seasons at a strike rate of 148.9. Amit Mishra has taken the most wickets for them, 106. The highest score made for the franchise is KL Rahul's 152* against Punjab Kings in 2026, and their highest total 264/2 against Punjab Kings in 2026. They have played more matches in Delhi than anywhere else, 94.
