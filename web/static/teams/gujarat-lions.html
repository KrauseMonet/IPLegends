Gujarat Lions

The franchise

Gujarat Lions played 2 seasons of the IPL, from 2016 to 2017. The franchise no longer plays. Across 30 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 13 and lost 17, winning 43% of the matches that had a result; one of those wins and losses came in a super over after a tie.

They never reached a final. They reached the knockouts in 1 season of 2. Their most wins in a season came in 2016, 9 from 16 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is Suresh Raina, with 841 runs in 2 seasons at a strike rate of 135.9. Dhawal Kulkarni took the most wickets for them, 21. The highest score made for the franchise is Suresh Raina's 84 against Kolkata Knight Riders in 2017, and their highest total 208/7 against Delhi Daredevils in 2017. They played more matches in Rajkot than anywhere else, 10.
