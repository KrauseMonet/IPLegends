Gujarat Titans

The franchise

Gujarat Titans have played 5 seasons of the IPL, from 2022 to 2026. Across 77 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 47 and lost 30, winning 61% of the matches that had a result.

They have won the title once (2022) and lost two finals (2023 and 2026). They reached the knockouts in 4 seasons of 5. Their most wins in a season came in 2022, 12 from 16 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is Shubman Gill, with 3,181 runs in 5 seasons at a strike rate of 152.6. Rashid Khan has taken the most wickets for them, 86. The highest score made for the franchise is Shubman Gill's 129 against Mumbai Indians in 2023, and their highest total 233/3 against Mumbai Indians in 2023. They have played more matches in Ahmedabad than anywhere else, 31.
