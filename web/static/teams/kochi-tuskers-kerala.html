Kochi Tuskers Kerala

The franchise

Kochi Tuskers Kerala played a single season of the IPL, in 2011. The franchise no longer plays. Across 14 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 6 and lost 8, winning 43% of the matches that had a result.

They never reached a final. They did not reach the knockouts.

Their leading run-scorer is Brendon McCullum, with 357 runs at a strike rate of 129.8. RP Singh took the most wickets for them, 13. The highest score made for the franchise is Brendon McCullum's 81 against Mumbai Indians in 2011, and their highest total 184/2 against Mumbai Indians in 2011. They played more matches in Kochi than anywhere else, 5.
