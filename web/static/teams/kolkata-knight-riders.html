Kolkata Knight Riders

The franchise

Kolkata Knight Riders have played 19 seasons of the IPL, from 2008 to 2026. Across 278 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 142 and lost 134, with 2 no results, winning 51% of the matches that had a result; five of those wins and losses came in a super over after a tie.

They have won the title three times (2012, 2014 and 2024) and lost one final (2021). They reached the knockouts in 8 seasons of 19. Their most wins in a season came in 2012, 12 from 17 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is Gautam Gambhir, with 3,035 runs in 7 seasons at a strike rate of 124.3. Sunil Narine has taken the most wickets for them, 207. The highest score made for the franchise is Brendon McCullum's 158* against Royal Challengers Bangalore in 2008, and their highest total 272/7 against Delhi Capitals in 2024. They have played more matches in Kolkata than anywhere else, 102.
