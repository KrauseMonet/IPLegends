The franchise

Lucknow Super Giants have played 5 seasons of the IPL, from 2022 to 2026. Across 72 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 34 and lost 37, with 1 no result, winning 48% of the matches that had a result; one of those wins and losses came in a super over after a tie.

They have never reached a final. They reached the knockouts in 2 seasons of 5. Their most wins in a season came in 2022, 9 from 15 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is Nicholas Pooran, with 1,615 runs in 4 seasons at a strike rate of 172.4. Ravi Bishnoi has taken the most wickets for them, 48. The highest score made for the franchise is Quinton de Kock's 140* against Kolkata Knight Riders in 2022, and their highest total 257/5 against Punjab Kings in 2023. They have played more matches in Lucknow than anywhere else, 28.
