Mumbai Indians

The franchise

Mumbai Indians have played 19 seasons of the IPL, from 2008 to 2026. Across 291 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 157 and lost 134, winning 54% of the matches that had a result; four of those wins and losses came in a super over after a tie.

They have won the title five times (2013, 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2020) and lost one final (2010). They reached the knockouts in 11 seasons of 19. Their most wins in a season came in 2013, 13 from 19 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is Rohit Sharma, with 6,159 runs in 16 seasons at a strike rate of 133.2. Jasprit Bumrah has taken the most wickets for them, 187. The highest score made for the franchise is Ryan Rickelton's 123* against Sunrisers Hyderabad in 2026, and their highest total 247/9 against Delhi Capitals in 2024. They have played more matches in Mumbai than anywhere else, 118.
