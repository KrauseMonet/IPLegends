Punjab Kings

The franchise

Punjab Kings have played 19 seasons of the IPL, from 2008 to 2026. The franchise has played as Kings XI Punjab (2008–2020) and Punjab Kings (2021–2026). Across 278 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 129 and lost 146, with 3 no results, winning 47% of the matches that had a result; four of those wins and losses came in a super over after a tie.

They have never won the title, and have been runners-up twice (2014 and 2025)). They reached the knockouts in 3 seasons of 19. Their most wins in a season came in 2014, 12 from 17 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is KL Rahul, with 2,548 runs in 4 seasons at a strike rate of 139.8. Arshdeep Singh has taken the most wickets for them, 111. The highest score made for the franchise is KL Rahul's 132* against Royal Challengers Bangalore in 2020, and their highest total 265/4 against Delhi Capitals in 2026. They have played more matches in Chandigarh than anywhere else, 61.
