Rajasthan Royals

The franchise

Rajasthan Royals have played 17 seasons of the IPL, from 2008 to 2026. They did not take part in 2016 and 2017. Across 251 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 125 and lost 124, with 2 no results, winning 50% of the matches that had a result; four of those wins and losses came in a super over after a tie.

They have won the title once (2008) and lost one final (2022). They reached the knockouts in 7 seasons of 17. Their most wins in a season came in 2008, 13 from 16 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is Sanju Samson, with 4,027 runs in 11 seasons at a strike rate of 141.2. Jofra Archer has taken the most wickets for them, 82. The highest score made for the franchise is Yashasvi Jaiswal's 124 against Mumbai Indians in 2023, and their highest total 243/8 against Sunrisers Hyderabad in 2026. They have played more matches in Jaipur than anywhere else, 66.
