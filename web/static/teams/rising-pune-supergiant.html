Rising Pune Supergiant

The franchise

Rising Pune Supergiant played 2 seasons of the IPL, from 2016 to 2017. The franchise has played as Rising Pune Supergiants (2016) and Rising Pune Supergiant (2017). The franchise no longer plays. Across 30 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 15 and lost 15, winning 50% of the matches that had a result.

They never won the title, and were runners-up once (2017)). They reached the knockouts in 1 season of 2. Their most wins in a season came in 2017, 10 from 16 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is Ajinkya Rahane, with 862 runs in 2 seasons at a strike rate of 122.8. Jaydev Unadkat took the most wickets for them, 24. The highest score made for the franchise is Ben Stokes' 103* against Gujarat Lions in 2017, and their highest total 195/3 against Gujarat Lions in 2016. They played more matches in Pune than anywhere else, 11.
