Royal Challengers Bengaluru

The franchise

Royal Challengers Bengaluru have played 19 seasons of the IPL, from 2008 to 2026. The franchise has played as Royal Challengers Bangalore (2008–2023) and Royal Challengers Bengaluru (2024–2026). Across 286 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 145 and lost 138, with 3 no results, winning 51% of the matches that had a result; three of those wins and losses came in a super over after a tie.

They have won the title twice (2025 and 2026) and lost three finals (2009, 2011 and 2016). They reached the knockouts in 11 seasons of 19. Their most wins in a season came in 2025, 11 from 15 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is Virat Kohli, with 9,336 runs in 19 seasons at a strike rate of 134.8. Yuzvendra Chahal has taken the most wickets for them, 139. The highest score made for the franchise is Chris Gayle's 175* against Pune Warriors in 2013, and their highest total 263/5 against Pune Warriors in 2013. They have played more matches in Bangalore than anywhere else, 61.
