Sunrisers Hyderabad

The franchise

Sunrisers Hyderabad have played 14 seasons of the IPL, from 2013 to 2026. Across 211 matches in the ball-by-ball record they won 103 and lost 107, with 1 no result, winning 49% of the matches that had a result; four of those wins and losses came in a super over after a tie.

They have won the title once (2016) and lost two finals (2018 and 2024). They reached the knockouts in 8 seasons of 14. Their most wins in a season came in 2016, 11 from 17 matches.

Their leading run-scorer is David Warner, with 4,014 runs in 7 seasons at a strike rate of 142.6. Bhuvneshwar Kumar has taken the most wickets for them, 157. The highest score made for the franchise is Abhishek Sharma's 141 against Punjab Kings in 2025, and their highest total 287/3 against Royal Challengers Bengaluru in 2024. They have played more matches in Hyderabad than anywhere else, 70.
